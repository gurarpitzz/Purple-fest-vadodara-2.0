# PurpleFest Visual Language

## 1. Signature Motifs
- **The Trinity of Inclusion (3 Hearts + Horizon Ribbon)**: Inspired by the official PurpleFest Vadodara emblem, rendered in Three.js as three floating sculptural crystalline/gold torus-heart forms orbiting a central luminous inclusion prism.
- **Gaekwad Royal Modernism**: Subtle architectural arches and golden light beams honoring Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III's 140-year legacy of inclusive education in Baroda, fused with futuristic assistive-tech geometry.
- **6-Layer 2.5D Depth System (`epic-design`)**:
  - `depth-0`: Far atmospheric nebula & volumetric purple void
  - `depth-1`: Screen-blended amethyst & gold glow orbs (`mix-blend-mode: screen`)
  - `depth-2`: Floating companion geometric badges & ghost stroke typography
  - `depth-3`: Hero 3D sculptures, interactive zone holocards, and visual showcases
  - `depth-4`: High-contrast accessible HTML typography, interactive filters, and registration controls
  - `depth-5`: Foreground micro-particles and interactive magnetic cursor aura
