Purple Fest Vadodara 2027

Celebrating diversity

Date: 14 – 16 January 2027
Fee: ₹2200
Venue: MSU Pavilion & Sayaji Nagar Gruh, Vadodara
Register FAQ

Registration for the Purple Fest Vadodara 2027 gives you access to the International Conference on Inclusive Education (in partnership with GIEN & TISS), Purple Dialogue, Assistive Tech Expo, Paralympic Arena, and the Inaugural and Closing ceremonies on 14, 15, and 16 January 2027. Join educators, innovators, policymakers, and advocates from around the world in Sanskari Nagari Vadodara as we explore new horizons in inclusive education, universal design, and disability rights across all 21 Benchmark Disabilities (RPwD Act 2016).

Once registered, you will receive a confirmation and QR by email. Present the QR at the reception desk to receive your conference kit and ID card.

Tea and lunch are included on 14, 15, and 16 January 2027. After registration, you may attend all events on the three days.

The venue is accessible, and volunteers will be available to assist you. Braille materials and Sign Language Interpreters will also be available.

Payments and invoicing for the Purple Fest Vadodara 2027 are handled by the Purple Fest Vadodara Organising Secretariat — Praangan (Vadodara) & We Are Together (Brotherhood).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Purple Fest Vadodara 2027

1. Where can I find the schedule of events at the Purple Fest Vadodara 2027?
You can view the complete schedule on the Schedule page, which lists all events taking place during the three-day festival on 14, 15, and 16 January 2027 across all 6 Experience Zones in Vadodara.
2. When should I arrive at the venue?
The festival begins on 14 January 2027 at 9:00 AM. All participants are requested to arrive on time and be seated in the Main Auditorium (Sayaji Nagar Gruh / MSU Pavilion) by 9:30 AM for the Inaugural Function.
3. How can I participate in events at the Purple Fest Vadodara 2027?
During the three-day festival, several parallel events will take place, including the International Conference on Inclusive Education (GIEN & TISS Conclave), Cultural Performances (Purple Kaleidoscope), Sports Events (Para-Sports Arena), Assistive Tech Expo, and Film Screenings with Audio Description and Indian Sign Language Interpretation. After registering, you are welcome to attend any of these events.
4. Who can I contact if I need assistance during the festival?
Trained Sighted-Guide & Inclusion Volunteers will be present at the venue to assist you at any time. You can also reach out to the Reception & Accessibility Desk at the main venue entrance.
5. Is accommodation provided by the organisers?
No, the organisers do not provide free accommodation. Participants are requested to arrange their own stay in Vadodara. Curated accessible hotel recommendations near Sayaji Baug, Alkapuri, and Fatehgunj are available in the Venue Accommodation Information section below.
6. Are all events held at the same venue, and is it well connected by public transport?
Yes, all events will be held across the barrier-free Vadodara Festival Corridor anchored at the Maharaja Sayajirao University (MSU) Pavilion, Sayaji Nagar Gruh, and Sayaji Baug in Vadodara, Gujarat. The venue is 10–15 minutes from Vadodara Junction Railway Station and 20 minutes from Vadodara Airport (BDQ), and is well connected by accessible city transport, cabs, auto-rickshaws, and dedicated festival electric buggy shuttles.
7. When does the festival conclude?
The festival concludes with the adoption of the Vadodara Declaration and the Valedictory Function on 16 January 2027 at 5:00 PM.
8. Is there a registration fee for attending the Purple Fest?
Yes, the registration fee is ₹2200 for the three-day festival (14–16 January 2027).
9. Are meals provided during the festival?
Yes, registered participants will receive coupons for complimentary lunch and tea on 14, 15, and 16 January 2027.
10. How can I join the Inclusive Heritage Walk & Rally on 14 January 2027?
You may assemble at Gate No. 1 of Sayaji Baug / MSU Pavilion on 14 January 2027 at 9:00 AM to take part in the Inclusive Uttarayan Rally & Heritage Walk.
11. Do I need to register separately for sports events like Goalball, Boccia, or Wheelchair Basketball?
No separate registration is required. Once registered for the Purple Fest, you are welcome to attend and enjoy all adaptive sports matches and Paralympic Champion sessions in Zone 04.
12. How can I pay the registration fee?
You can pay the registration fee online via UPI, Net Banking, or Debit/Credit Card. After completing the payment, you will receive a confirmation email with a QR code. Please present this QR code at the Reception Desk at the venue to collect your festival kit, lunch coupons, and ID card.
13. Can I visit the exhibition stalls at the Purple Pavilion?
Yes, once registered, you can freely visit all 100+ assistive technology, startup, CSR, and DPO artisan exhibition stalls at the Purple Pavilion (Zone 01).
14. Why is there a coupon code option in the registration form?
Some institutions, universities, and CSR sponsors have covered the registration fees for their students, grassroots DPO delegates, or panellists. Those participants have been provided with a coupon code by the organisers to waive the fee. If you do not have a coupon code, you can simply leave that field blank.
15. Who is organising Purple Fest Vadodara 2027?
The festival is jointly organised by Praangan (Vadodara) and We Are Together (Brotherhood, New Delhi), in collaboration with the Global Inclusive Education Network (GIEN), Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS Mumbai), Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda (MSU), Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Govt. of India), and the Office of the State Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities, Government of Goa as Knowledge & Legacy Partner.
16. Can we register at the venue on the day of the event?
Online registration in advance is strongly recommended so we can prepare your Braille/large-print kit and RPwD accommodations. Limited assisted registration counters will be available at the venue reception desk subject to capacity.
