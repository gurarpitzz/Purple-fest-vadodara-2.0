01 / National Mandate & Vision

Why Purple & Viksit Bharat @ 2047.

3D VECTOR // ACCESSIBLE MOBILITY

01 // ARCHITECTURE OF CAPABILITY
Scroll to illuminate each line.

Globally, the colour Purple symbolises dignity, creative authority, and the economic contribution of over 1.3 billion Persons with Disabilities.

A developed India by 2047 (Viksit Bharat) cannot be engineered while leaving 2.68 crore citizens at the margins of infrastructure and industry.

Purple Fest Vadodara 2027 rejects outdated welfare narratives in favour of industrial design, indigenous assistive hardware, and high-performance athletics.

Following landmark editions in Goa and at Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi, Every delegate registers at a standard ₹2,200 pass fee to sustain world-class barrier-free infrastructure.

Pillar I / Deep-Tech

Indigenous Assistive R&D

Make-in-India bionic prosthetics, refreshable Braille hardware, and AI computer-vision wearables built by Indian startups.

Pillar II / Urban Systems

Universal Civic Design

Harmonised barrier-free municipal codes, tactile transit corridors, and zero-step public architecture across Gujarat.

Pillar III / Sports Science

Paralympic Powerhouse

Grassroots para-athletic talent identification, biomechanics labs, and adaptive sports infrastructure for 2028 & 2032 Games.

Pillar IV / Economy

DEI Workforce & Enterprise

Direct corporate recruitment, Divyangjan entrepreneurship stalls, and assistive workplace automation across industry.

03 / Athletic Mastery & World Records

Indian Paralympic Icons.

3D VECTOR // PARA-SPORTS IN MOTION

03 // HALL OF PERFORMANCE
Click any athlete row to inspect technical classification & records.

India's historic surge at the Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024 Paralympic Games redefined how the nation perceives human capability, biomechanics, and elite sport.

Purple Fest Vadodara 2027 hosts India's premier Paralympic medalists for live technical clinics, adaptive sports demonstrations, and keynote dialogues at the MSU Sports Complex.

CLASSIFICATION: SH1 · RIFLE 10M & 50M Padma Shri · Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna

Avani Lekhara

The first Indian woman in history to win two Paralympic Gold medals. Competing in the SH1 wheelchair shooting category, she set a new Paralympic Record of 249.7 in the R2 10m Air Rifle Standing SH1 at Paris 2024, defending her Tokyo 2020 crown with unmatched mechanical precision.

Career Milestones & Records
  • Gold Medal — Paris 2024 Paralympics (10m Air Rifle SH1 · PR 249.7)
  • Gold Medal — Tokyo 2020 Paralympics (10m Air Rifle SH1 · WR Equalled)
  • Bronze Medal — Tokyo 2020 Paralympics (50m Rifle 3 Positions SH1)
  • Gold Medal — 2022 Asian Para Games, Hangzhou
Purple Fest Vadodara 2027 Feature

04 / Statutory Framework · The Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016

The 21 Benchmark Disabilities.

3D VECTOR // 21 RPwD CLASSIFICATIONS

04 // STATUTORY SCHEDULE

In 2016, India expanded statutory recognition from 7 conditions to 21 Benchmark Disabilities under the RPwD Act.

Purple Fest Vadodara 2027 is architecturally and programmatically engineered across all 21 classifications — ensuring specialized assistive technology, clinical panels, and environmental design for every category.
